The case and the conversation gap

Strengths to recognise

  • Dedicated and committed
  • Strong technical knowledge
  • Well-liked by colleagues
  • Reliable in routine operations
  • Positive towards learning

SJS gap to clarify

  • Independence at grade
  • Strategic thinking and prioritisation
  • Leading and influencing
  • Operating with less direction
  • Sustaining improvement over time

The aim is not to label the person. It is to create a shared, evidence-based understanding of role expectations and next steps.

EnABLE: the structure for the conversation

Conversation example

En + A: open clearly, then listen

“Susie, I want us to have an honest discussion about the Manager role, your career and the support ahead.”

“I value your commitment, technical knowledge and reliability. I also need to be clear about the expectations of the grade and where there is a continuing gap.”

“Some decisions may already be determined, but I want to hear your perspective and discuss the way forward respectfully.”

How do you assess your current work against the Manager SJS?

Which expectations can you perform independently?

Where do you still need regular direction?

What feedback have you heard repeatedly?

Listen for the staff’s understanding, evidence, concerns and aspirations.

From conversation to commitment

Sample GROW actions linked to SJS

G · Goal

Agree the SJS-aligned scope in the revised role and identify 1–2 priority capabilities to strengthen.

R · Reality

Document strengths, recurring gaps, examples, support already provided and the staff member’s perspective.

O · Options

Select appropriate support such as guided assignments, mentoring, coaching, formal learning or suitable pathways.

W · Way forward

Record SMART actions, owners, evidence of progress and scheduled check-ins. Loop back using CAST.

Workshop principle: define actions together, but do not present a decided regrading outcome as negotiable.
Key takeaway

Close with clarity, dignity and follow-through

The staff member should leave knowing. Tick each one off as the group agrees.

EnABLE makes the conversation safe and clear. GROW turns clarity into shared, practical action.
